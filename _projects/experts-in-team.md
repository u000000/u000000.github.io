## Overview
This project addresses the industrial challenge presented by the Schmalz initiative: a solution integrating vacuum gripping technology with drones for payload pick-and-place operations. The objective is to design and prototype a drone that can autonomously retrieve and deliver boxes from shelves to defined landing points with as little human intervention as possible. Traditional handling methods are quite challenged in environments where boxes are stored in vertical stacks and space is limited, especially when retrieving boxes from hard-to-reach locations. The proposed prototype solution is based on a drone equipped with a suction gripper that can grasp boxes from the side and an active balancing system, enabled by an actuated counterweight, to stabilize flight in tight spaces. A centralized database with unique location identifiers for each box is maintained where users can search, select, and retrieve boxes through a UI.
