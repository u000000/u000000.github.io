## Overview
The linear stage module is a device designed for precise positioning of components with varying dimensions and masses along a single axis. It is commonly used in research and educational laboratories for accurate sample positioning under microscopes. The system can also be utilized for positioning optical components and for other applications requiring high-precision movement of materials or tools.

Two linear stage modules can be combined into a single system, enabling positioning in two axes (X and Y).
