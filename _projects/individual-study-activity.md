## Problem Formulation
The project challenges student to work on the task individually - to efficiently manage time. The main task will be creation of the VTOL (Vertical Take-off and Landing) aircraft. The student is supposed to state the requirements and do the research to find the most suitable type of VTOL. The UAV needs to be built and configured. The final step of the project will be the flight which will verify the whole process of design and manufacturing done by the student.

## Overview
The purpose of this project is to build a VTOL that could be used for long-range inspections with the possibility of performing close "helicopter-like" approaches to specific objects during the mission. This means it has to be efficient and fast to reach far destinations, but also stable and controllable to fly close to objects under inspection. The VTOL is prepared to be equipped with cameras and sensors in the future to enable scanning and other functionalities.

It was an Individual Study Activity which is mainly based on hardware, not only software. This means that I couldn't allocate a lot of resources. This is my passion, and I wanted to keep the aircraft after the project, so all the parts were bought by me. This is why I sometimes had to make decisions that were not ideal for the prototype I created but were the most optimal from both financial and performance points of view.
