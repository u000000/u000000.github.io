## Overview
Developed a lightweight tail-sitter VTOL drone for search-and-rescue applications, capable of transitioning between vertical and fixed-wing flight. The project involved aerodynamic design, structural analysis, CAD modeling, manufacturing, and flight testing, with a focus on lightweight construction, stability, and integrated communication capabilities.

University of Southern Denmark
